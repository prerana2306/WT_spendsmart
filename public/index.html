Engineered for College Life

Not just basic CRUD. SpendSmart includes 10 purpose-built fintech features designed to solve genuine student money struggles.

AI Smart Saving Assistant

Ask questions like "Can I afford a ₹2,500 jacket?" or "How much can I spend this week?" and receive answers computed against your live balance and upcoming obligations.

Needs vs Wants Analyzer

Auto-classifies food, transport, and tuition as Needs while spotlighting impulse food delivery and shopping as Wants with interactive 50/30/20 recommendations.

Purchase Decision Calculator

Tests item affordability while reserving cushion for upcoming semester fees and essential bills, calculating an exact safe waiting period if risky.

Financial Health Score

Transparent 100-point score evaluating savings consistency, budget adherence, spending velocity, needs/wants ratio, and goal progress with actionable tips.

Expense Anomaly Detection

Detects statistical spending surges (e.g. food spending up 100% vs historical 30-day baseline) and single outlier transactions without false alarms.

Split Bills with Friends

Group expense splitting for hostel flatmates and dining outings with equal or custom shares, live balances (who owes whom), and recorded settlements.
